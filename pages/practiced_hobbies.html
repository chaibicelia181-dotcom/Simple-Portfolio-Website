The things that keep me sane outside of university

Piano

I am naturally a curious person, always trying different hobbies until I eventually fall in love with one. Since I love music, I naturally wanted to play an instrument. I first tried to pick up the guitar, but I soon realised that it wasn't my cup of tea.

I then decided to give piano a try, and it was honestly the best decision I ever made. I genuinely fell in love with the instrument.

At first, I struggled to coordinate my hands and almost gave up. But after months of self-teaching — because yes, I never took any classes, I just taught myself everything I know — I finally learned to enjoy the beauty of this instrument.

I reached a level where I could let myself be transported by the music while playing, without worrying about where the next note would be. That is one of the best feelings a person can experience.

Crochet

Onto the next hobby. I picked up crochet a few years ago.

It wasn't completely random: my friend at the time used to crochet a lot, and I thought it looked so cool, which made me want to learn. Just like with piano, I taught myself everything I needed to know.

I followed online tutorials and, without even noticing, I slowly started to get better. I began my crochet journey with small projects like pouches and bag charms.

As I became more confident and experienced, I tackled more challenging pieces. The creations I am most proud of are my green dragonfly jumper and my crochet pillow.

Click here if you want to take a glimpse at them.

Crochet is a relaxing activity for me; I love to have a movie or TV show playing in the background while I crochet. It helps me wind down after a long day.

Reading

Last but not least, there is reading. I have always been more or less a bookworm. My father, who is a big reader and a good writer himself, was the one who first encouraged me to read.

Fun fact: during Covid, when everything was closed and we couldn't go book shopping, he made me read one of his books — the full text of Les Misérables by Victor Hugo. The book was approximately 1,600 pages long. It took me a while to finish it, but I eventually did.

I don't have one specific favourite genre. I read a bit of everything, from fantasy and thrillers to classics. I have even dedicated a whole page to this hobby, where I share some of my recommendations and books I would like to read.
